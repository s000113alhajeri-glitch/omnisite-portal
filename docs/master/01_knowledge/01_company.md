# 1. Company

## What OmniSite is
A retrofit, read-mostly **operations-intelligence layer for UAE SME batch factories** (chemicals, detergents, cosmetics; Dubai and Sharjah). It shows the owner what is really happening in the factory and turns it into AED, without taking control of the machines. **[decision]**

- Tagline (2nd-gen offer): "Make your factory smarter with a digital twin." **[decision]**
- Rule of three: 1 business-operations audit, 2 software (owner dashboard), 3 hardware (IoT). **[decision]**
- Positioning: for SMEs, not against Siemens / Honeywell. Audit-first: never lead with sensors, AI or the digital twin. **[decision]**
- OmniSite observes. It never starts or stops machines. **[decision]**

## Stages of the offer **[decision]**
| Stage | What |
|---|---|
| 0 | Site survey ("try us") |
| 1 | Plug-and-play: connect what already has data |
| 2 | Add IoT to equipped machines |
| 3 | Machines to replace |

MVP = 1 machine (mixing tank) + 1 dashboard, sold as prototype-as-a-service, then the audit is upsold. **[decision]**

- End goal of the 90-day pilot: "pre-seed ready" (pilot funded and completed, pipeline waiting). Earlier pitch figure: pre-seed AED 500k. **[assumption: not a fixed raise]**
