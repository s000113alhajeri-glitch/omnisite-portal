# Pilot timeline and KPIs

## 90 days (her plan) **[decision]**

## KPIs vs baseline (mixer)
- Batches passed first time vs re-mixed vs rejected.
- Time to pass QC; extra re-mix time; "could have made N batches".
- Material used (kg) and added after a failed QC.
- Equipment stop minutes by reason; money lost (AED, after prices are set).
- QC limits: record 2 weeks first, then propose limits (never invent them).
