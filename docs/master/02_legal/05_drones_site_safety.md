# Drones and site safety

- Indoor drone rules in the UAE (GCAA / DCAA) and factory permission: **not checked yet**. Must be checked before any drone R&D on a site.
- Site work: Chemex maintenance approval before wiring; drive Modbus settings read-only; work with the drive off; old panels checked first.
- PPE and chemical-area rules: ask the factory's safety officer before each visit.
