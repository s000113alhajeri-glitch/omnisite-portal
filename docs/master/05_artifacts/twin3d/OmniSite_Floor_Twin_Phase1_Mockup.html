What Phase 1 does

The audit engineer uses the current CRM on his phone plus a laser distance meter. For each hall he records the size. For each machine and power point he scans its QR label, takes the nameplate photo (AI fills in the details, he confirms), and types its exact position in cm from corner A1. The CRM draws the floor map on a 50×50 cm grid, finds the nearest power point and calculates the cable length. Everything is saved on the factory, so the next engineer sees it all.

Mockup only: nothing is built or changed in the live CRM yet. All names and numbers below are examples (a home room with a laptop and a TV).

How I'll build it

1

Halls + exact positions

Planned

Add “Halls” to the factory (name, length, width in cm, wall direction from the compass, corner A1 photo). Each equipment record gets: hall, position from A1, size, height, all in cm. The existing Site survey → Equipment form gets the 4 position fields. The map is drawn from these numbers.

2

Power points

Planned

Same form, type “Power point” (socket, panel), labels P-001, P-002… with position and height.

3

Nearest power + cable length

Planned

Tap a machine → nearest power point + route length. Engineer can tap the real route; CRM adds horizontal cm + up/down drops + your margin %. Saved into the installation plan.

4

AI reads the nameplate

Planned

After the nameplate photo, an AI vision service suggests name, make + model, rating. Engineer taps Correct or edits. If unknown, he names it. Confirmed answers are saved in an object library.

5

3D twin dashboard

Planned

From the saved cm positions and sizes, the CRM draws every item as a 3D block on the 50 cm floor grid (round for tanks, boxes for panels and displays, steps for stairs). Tap → photo, nameplate details, nearest power, cable route, connected or not. 360° spin. Later: “View in AR” on iPhone (USDZ) and Android with ARCore (GLB), and VR headsets through the browser.

6

Walk mode (next)

Next phase

Camera on while walking: AI spots items, asks you to confirm or name them, and guides you closer to scan the QR and nameplate. Exact cm from the walk itself needs an iPhone app with LiDAR; until then the laser meter gives the cm.

7

Test at home, then Chemex

Planned

Practice factory first (laptop, TV, sockets), check every cm and the cable number with a tape, then Mixer 1 at Chemex.
