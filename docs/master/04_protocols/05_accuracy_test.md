# Protocol 5: Position accuracy test

**[proposal, not yet run]**

1. Choose A1 at a fixed corner; measure the hall in cm.
2. Place 4 big A3 markers (A-001…A-004) on tripods, one near each wall. Measure each from A1 with the laser meter. Nothing is stuck on the walls.
3. Label the machines (M-…), scan, nameplate photo, measure from A1.
4. Walk slowly: phone upright at chest height, side-to-side sweeps. Point at each marker / label for 1 s. 2 laser shots at each corner. Finish at A1.
5. Choose 10 check points not used in the scan; measure each by hand from A1.
6. Compare with the twin. Proposed pass: 9 of 10 within 1 cm, none worse than 3 cm.
7. Repeat 3 times. Remove the tripods.

Home version (Monday test): one room, A1, 2–4 markers on chairs, laptop / TV / socket labels, 5 tape check points.
