## The 8 audit points
1. Where material is lost, wasted, or overused
2. Where batches are remixed, delayed, rejected, or inconsistent
3. How operators record quantities, timing, temperature, mixing, and quality checks
4. Where people wait for approvals, information, materials, or equipment
5. The actual time for each batch step and each handover
6. Energy, water, raw-material, labour, rework, and downtime cost
7. Which equipment already has data, sensors, PLC/SCADA, scales, or manual logs
8. Who owns each decision: operator, supervisor, quality, maintenance, production manager, finance

## Baseline report areas
| Area | Business impact | OmniSite opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Material use | Waste and cost leakage | Digital batch records and variance alerts |
| Mixing | Rework and quality risk | Timers, operator prompts, process monitoring |
| Batch tracking | Delayed investigations and weak traceability | Real-time digital logs |
| Downtime | Lost production capacity | Downtime categorisation and alerts |
| Energy | Hidden operational cost | Sub-metering and energy-per-batch insight |

Pick one top pain point for the pilot proposal.
