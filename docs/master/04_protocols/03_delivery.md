# Protocol 3: Readiness and delivery

Source: CRM readiness gates. **[verified]**

1. Survey approved (all 6 engineering files approved).
2. **Before delivery** card: scope meeting (date, link, "Confirmed by" name or "Changes needed"), down payment (AED, date, proof file), install crew booked (count, names, first / last day).
3. Delivery engineer confirms every asset is **In stock** and sets the delivery date.
4. Scan every asset QR **before dispatch**. The last scan alerts the install engineer: red "Delivery left … Go to the site now".
5. Scan every asset QR **after delivery** at the factory. Both scans are required before Send for review.
